Tiling is priced on the area, the size and type of tile and the preparation the walls or floor need. Large format and patterned tiles take more setting out than standard ones.

What the Work Covers

Tiling looks simple until the lines start to wander or the cuts end up in the wrong place. We set out every wall and floor before we start, so the finished job looks planned rather than squeezed in. We can also tile as part of a full kitchen or bathroom job, so the walls are prepared, tiled and finished by the same team without waiting on anyone else.

  • Preparing walls and floors so they are flat and sound
  • Setting out so cuts fall in the right place
  • Wall tiling in kitchens and bathrooms
  • Floor tiling throughout the house
  • Splashbacks
  • Grouting and sealing
The Process

What to Expect

  1. VisitWe check the walls or floor and talk through the tiles and layout you have in mind.
  2. Your quoteA free quote covering preparation, tiling and grouting.
  3. PreparationSurfaces are made flat and sound, because tiles show every lump underneath.
  4. Setting out and tilingWe set the tiles out so the cuts fall in the right places, then fix them level and straight.
  5. Grouting and finishingGrout and sealant finish the job neatly, and we clean up when we are done.
