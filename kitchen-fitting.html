What the Work Covers

The kitchen is the room most people use most, so it has to work as well as it looks. Because we are builders as well as fitters, we can deal with the walls, floors and openings around the kitchen in the same job.

  • Stripping out the old kitchen
  • Making good walls and levelling floors
  • Taking out walls to open the room up
  • Fitting units, worktops and splashbacks
  • Tiling and flooring
  • Plastering and decorating to finish the room
The Process
