What You'll Pay and How We Quote

Plastering and rendering are priced on the area, the state of what is underneath and how much preparation it needs. A quick skim over sound walls is a very different job to hacking off and starting again.

What the Work Covers

Good plastering is the difference between a room that looks finished and one that never quite looks right, however good the paint. Outside, render protects the walls as well as tidying up the look of the house.

  • Skimming over sound walls and ceilings
  • Hacking off and re-plastering where the old plaster has blown
  • Plasterboarding new walls and ceilings
  • Patch repairs after building work
  • External rendering
  • Preparing walls ready for decorating
The Process

What to Expect
