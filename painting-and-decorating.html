What You'll Pay and How We Quote

Decorating is priced on the size of the job and how much preparation the surfaces need. Walls that need filling and sanding take longer than ones that just need a fresh coat.

What the Work Covers

Decorating is usually the last thing people see and the first thing they notice. Most of a good finish comes from preparation, so that is where we put the time in. We protect your floors and furniture, take care over the edges and leave every room clean, so the only thing you notice afterwards is the finish.

  • Filling cracks, holes and dents
  • Sanding and preparing woodwork
  • Painting walls and ceilings
  • Doors, skirting boards and frames
  • Exterior walls and woodwork
  • Decorating after building and plastering work
