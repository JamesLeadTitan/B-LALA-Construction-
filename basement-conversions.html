What You'll Pay and How We Quote

Basement work varies more than almost any other job, because so much depends on the condition of the space, how damp it is and how much headroom there is. We come out and look before we put a figure on anything.

After the visit you get a free quote that explains what the conversion involves and what's included, from keeping the water out to the finished room.

Call us on 020 3962 0684 or use the form at the top of the page to arrange a visit.

What the Work Covers

A good basement conversion feels like part of the house, not a cellar with a carpet thrown down. That comes down to getting it dry, getting the light and ventilation right and finishing it properly.

  • Clearing out and assessing the space
  • Damp control so the room stays dry
  • Forming walls, floors and ceilings
  • Boarding and plastering
  • Flooring and decorating
  • Making good the stairs and access
The Process

What to Expect

  1. Look at the spaceWe check the headroom, the walls and floor and any signs of damp so we know exactly what we are working with.
  2. A quote for the whole jobWe explain what the conversion needs and price it as one job, so there are no surprises halfway through.
  3. Keeping it dryGetting the space dry comes first. A basement room is only worth having if it stays dry year round.
  4. Building the roomWalls, floors and ceilings are formed, then the space is boarded, plastered and made ready for decorating.
  5. FinishingFlooring, decorating and the final details go in, and we walk through the finished room with you.
