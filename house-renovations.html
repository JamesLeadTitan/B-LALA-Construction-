House Renovations in Walthamstow

Quotes

What You'll Pay and How We Quote

Every renovation is different, so we don't work from a price list. We come out, walk round the house with you and look at what you want doing, room by room. Then we put together a quote that sets out the work, the order we'll do it in and what's included.

The visit and the quote are free. If anything changes once the walls are opened up, we stop, show you what we've found and talk it through before any extra work goes ahead. Nothing gets added to the job without you agreeing to it first.

Call us on 020 3962 0684 or use the form at the top of the page to arrange a visit.

What the Work Covers

A renovation can be as simple as updating a few tired rooms or as big as taking a house back to the brickwork and starting again. Either way, we plan the work so each trade follows on from the last without gaps.

  • Strip-outs and clearing the house back to a workable shell
  • Knocking through walls and fitting steels to open up rooms
  • Replacing damaged joists, floors and ceilings
  • Re-plastering and rendering, inside and out
  • Kitchen and bathroom fitting as part of the wider job
  • Tiling, flooring, and painting and decorating to finish
The Process

What to Expect

  1. Survey visitWe look at the whole property, not just the room you called about, so we can spot problems early and plan the work in a sensible order.
  2. A clear quoteYou get a quote that breaks the job down into stages, so you can see where the work and the money are going.
  3. Strip-out and structural workWe clear out what needs to go and deal with anything structural first, from knocking through walls to fitting steels.
  4. First and second fixWalls, floors and ceilings are made good, then the house is put back together ready for the finishes.
  5. Finishing tradesPlastering, tiling, flooring and decorating are done by the same team, so there is no waiting around for the next trade to turn up.
  6. Walk-round and handoverWe go round the finished job with you and sort out anything that isn't right before we call it done.
