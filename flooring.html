What the Work Covers

Flooring is often the last trade into a room, and it shows up every problem left underneath. We check and prepare the subfloor first, so the new floor sits flat and stays that way. Where flooring is part of a bigger job, such as an extension or a kitchen, we plan it as the final stage so it is not damaged by the work going on around it.

  • Lifting old flooring
  • Levelling and repairing subfloors
  • Laying wood and tiled floors
  • Neat edges at walls and doorways
  • Thresholds and trims
  • Flooring as part of a wider renovation or extension
